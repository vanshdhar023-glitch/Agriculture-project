About the Project

Clearer mandi(market) information for India's agricultural community.

Fasal Drishti is a project exploring how market prices, crop comparisons, nearby mandi discovery and price forecasts can come together in one useful workspace for farmers and traders.

The prototype combines Agrimarknet reference records with clearly marked demo samples. Forecasts and sample records are for demonstration and should not be treated as live trading advice.
